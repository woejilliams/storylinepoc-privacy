Storyline POC: Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026

Storyline POC is a prototype app for testing a new news-story format with KARE 11 staff. It does not ask you to sign in or create an account, and it does not collect, sell or share any personal information. The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and it does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos. All stories and images are built into the app, which makes no network requests of its own. Your settings and any feedback you enter with the Report button are saved only on your device and are removed when you delete the app. If you tap a link, such as a source article on kare11.com or a photo credit on Wikimedia Commons, it opens in your browser. If you share a story, iOS passes it to the app you choose. Those sites and apps have their own privacy policies. Because the app is distributed through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share your tester name and email, crash reports, basic usage information (such as installs and sessions) and any feedback you send through TestFlight with the developer, as described in Apple's TestFlight terms and privacy policy.
